Dressed for Everywhere.

We curate pieces that are bold, playful, and unmistakably feminine. Statement streetwear, delicate accessories, dreamy sleepwear, everyday must-haves. All carefully selected. All affordable.

From a small pop-up on Dome Road to a full online store serving customers across Ghana — this is Miss Tokyo.

The Miss Tokyo Story

We believe getting dressed should feel exciting, not stressful. That you should not have to choose between looking good and staying within budget. That fashion should be accessible — and fun — for every woman in Ghana and beyond.

Miss Tokyo was founded on this belief. We source every piece with care, test quality personally, and only stock what we would actually wear ourselves.

2021

It started with a pop-up

A small table, a rack of clothes, and a dream. Our first weekend market pop-up on Dome Road sold out in a day.

2022

First online store

We launched online, reached customers in Kumasi, Tema and Takoradi for the first time. Orders came in while we slept.

2023

1,000 customers

Crossed our first major milestone. Expanded the catalogue to include accessories, footwear, and activewear.

2025

New chapter

A fully redesigned online store, same-day delivery in Accra, and a team growing as fast as our community.
